TL;DR: Refocus anything in a video after capture. We render smooth, temporally consistent bokeh at user-specified dynamic focal planes.

Abstract

Video refocusing enables the focal plane of a shot to be adjusted after capture, even when the original video was recorded with arbitrary and time-varying focus. Existing video restoration methods assume static blur kernels and become unstable under dynamic focus, while video bokeh methods require restrictive inputs. We present Refocusing Any Video (RAV), a video refocusing framework that renders temporally smooth bokeh at user-specified dynamic focal planes. RAV introduces temporal sharpness and face-aware correspondence constraints for stable video refocusing under severe defocus. Experiments show stronger temporal consistency, identity preservation, and overall refocus quality than image refocusing baselines.
